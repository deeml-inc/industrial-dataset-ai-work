## Description

This June 2025 filing in the Government of Manitoba's Environmental Assessment and Licensing public registry concerns the water treatment plant at the Ruttan Mine, a former copper and zinc mine near Leaf Rapids in northern Manitoba whose drainage must be treated after closure. The 18 MB digital PDF carries the piping and instrumentation diagrams on pages 50 to 52 and 62 to 63.

The rest of the document is unusually rich for a P&ID source: it includes the plant operating manual, electrical, structural and mechanical drawings, and water quality monitoring data, so the drawings can be read together with how the plant is operated and what it achieves.

Like the other piping and instrumentation diagram sets in this collection, the document is a public record: it can be downloaded and read freely, but the drawings were prepared by engineering consultants who generally retain copyright, so check before republishing the drawings themselves.
