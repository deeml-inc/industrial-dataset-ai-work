## Description

The City of Salina, Kansas, publishes the final basis of design report for its wastewater treatment plant improvement project, dated May 2021. At 33 MB it is the largest document of the five P&ID sets, and it is a digital PDF with a mixed text layer.

The piping and instrumentation diagrams are on pages 114, 117, 119, 121, 126, 127, 132 and 134. Around them, the report holds the complete engineering narrative: an assessment of the existing plant's condition, an evaluation of alternatives, electrical diagrams and cost estimates. That makes it suited to work that links drawings to the design text that explains them, such as document question answering or extracting equipment lists.

Like the other piping and instrumentation diagram sets in this collection, the document is a public record: it can be downloaded and read freely, but the drawings were prepared by engineering consultants who generally retain copyright, so check before republishing the drawings themselves.
