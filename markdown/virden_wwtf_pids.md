# Virden Wastewater Treatment Facility Phase 2 P&I drawings

Scanned piping and instrumentation drawing set for phase 2 of the municipal wastewater treatment facility in Virden, Manitoba, filed in the provincial environmental assessment registry.

- **Domain:** Water and utilities
- **Asset / Process:** Water treatment and distribution
- **Modality:** Text and documents; Image
- **Task:** Other
- **Source Type:** Public records
- **Access:** Direct download
- **Size:** 3 MB scanned PDF; P&IDs on pages 5–14
- **Year:** 2019
- **Publisher:** Government of Manitoba

## Description

This drawing set was filed in April 2019 in the Government of Manitoba's Environmental Assessment and Licensing public registry for phase 2 of the Virden Wastewater Treatment Facility, a municipal plant in south-western Manitoba. The 3 MB PDF contains the piping and instrumentation drawings on pages 5 to 14 and nothing else, so it is the most compact of the five P&ID sets.

Unlike the other sets, the pages are scanned images rather than vector PDF with a text layer, which makes it a useful test case for optical recognition of tags, symbols and line work on legacy drawings.

Like the other piping and instrumentation diagram sets in this collection, the document is a public record: it can be downloaded and read freely, but the drawings were prepared by engineering consultants who generally retain copyright, so check before republishing the drawings themselves.
