## Description

The US Environmental Protection Agency published this design-build drawing set for the municipal wastewater treatment plant in Pahala, on the island of Hawaii, in July 2021. The 7 MB digital PDF holds piping and instrumentation diagrams on pages 9 to 25, preceded by legend, abbreviation and symbol sheets and a process schematic, and followed by site and civil plans.

The legend and symbol sheets make this the cleanest set in the group for machine reading, since the drawing conventions are defined in the same document. The source collection also notes it is a US federal government work, so it carries the fewest redistribution concerns of the five P&ID sets.

Like the other piping and instrumentation diagram sets in this collection, the document is a public record: it can be downloaded and read freely, but the drawings were prepared by engineering consultants who generally retain copyright, so check before republishing the drawings themselves.
